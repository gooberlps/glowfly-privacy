This Privacy Policy explains how Glowfly (“the App”) handles information when you use the App on Android. By using Glowfly, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Glowfly is a casual one-tap endless game. Privacy questions: micah.phillips88@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 On your device only

Stored locally on your phone:

  • Best score
  • Sound on/off
  • Haptics (vibration) on/off
  • Display name for the leaderboard (if you enter one)
  • Simple counters for ad frequency

2.2 Online leaderboard (optional)

If you submit a score, we may store:

  • Your display name (nickname you type)
  • Your score
  • An anonymous Firebase Authentication ID (no email/password required)

Stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The game works without using the leaderboard.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are shown via Google AdMob. Google and partners may process device identifiers (including advertising ID), IP address, approximate location from IP, device/app info, and ad interactions. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in apps

2.4 What we do not collect

  • Email/password login
  • Precise GPS
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone
  • Payment card details in the App

4. Sharing

We use Google Firebase (leaderboard) and Google AdMob (ads). We do not sell your personal information as a data broker.

5. Children

Glowfly is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The App may show ads. Contact us to request removal of a leaderboard entry involving a child.

6. Retention

  • Device data: until you clear app data or uninstall
  • Leaderboard: until deletion request or cleanup of abuse/inactivity
  • Ads: per Google’s policies

7. Your choices

  • Turn off sound or haptics in the App
  • Skip the leaderboard
  • Clear app storage in Android settings
  • Limit ad personalization in device Google/Ads settings
  • Email us to request leaderboard deletion

8. Security & international processing

We use HTTPS and Google services. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries under Google’s terms.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the “Last updated” date. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
